## 1. Executive summary
No path to unbacked value, theft, permanent freeze, false‑proof acceptance, or protocol‑level deanonymization was found across ~36k lines of Solidity + SP1 zkVM Rust. Every value‑moving op reduces to a Schnorr‑over‑blinding‑excess kernel; every hidden output is range‑proven; the subtle failure modes are explicitly guarded. Residual items (A1 BP+ IPA exponents box‑KAT‑gated; A3 deploy‑time gates; A4 DAO‑governed CollateralEngine + oracle; A5 host‑serializer parity; A6 public‑AMM periphery) are not defects in the frozen source. Recommend proceeding to freeze once the Section 8 checklist is green.

4.1 conservation kernel `ΣC_in − ΣC_out − net·H = excess·G` (BIP-340 over the residue x-only key; H NUMS-independent). 4.2 range binding to [0,2^64) (BP+ + classic); blind lanes add explicit BP+ on the fee leg. 4.3 double-spend + cross-lane retirement (secret-nk / leaf-bound nullifiers; bind_spent_note; fast-lane completeness + full-leaf binding + outpoint double-mint gate). 4.4 bridge-mint one-per-burn_id + asset preservation. 4.5 reflection validate-then-commit discipline (recomputed receipts, constant-product floor, refund-not-strand; farm reward ≤ reserve; cBTC value from confirmed output; burn-deposit provenance DAG). 4.6 Ethereum lane independently re-checked (no-inflation floor, k-non-decrease, pro-rata LP, escrow symmetry, generational rebase; no privileged drain). 4.7 ERC20 mint authority (MINTER == pool). 4.8 Bitcoin light client (heaviest-chain, per-branch retarget, canonical nBits, LE hash). 4.9 beacon Mode-B (head>prev_head, genesis pin, next_sync_committee None, zero-tolerant storage). 4.10 router non-custody (_recipeSalt). 4.11 least-privilege BtcCallExecutor/EthCallOutbox. 4.12 batch/blind inert (OP_SWAP_BLIND panic!; T_SWAP_BATCH folds nothing).

## 5. Findings
No Critical/High/Medium exploitable vulnerability. Residual assurance items:

- A4 — CollateralEngine (DAO-governed) + oracle: accepted trust posture, value invariants verified correct (no non-governance inflation; surplus draw fully gated; liquidation mints nothing). Action: document owner/feeds/ratios; confirm FarmController double-gated by the pool.

- A6 (info, lower) — plaintext public-AMM periphery is a separate value pool. Action (optional): focused pass if it holds material liquidity.

## 6. Cross-cutting (positive)
Defense in depth is real; subtle attack classes handled explicitly (modular-negative, shared-excess hole, 64-byte merkle-merge + CVE-2012-2459, >=-vs-> finality bootstrap, adaptor self-claim, front-run redirect, subset-witness censorship); immutability discipline (no owner/admin/upgrade/drain; disabled features proof-fatal/fold-nothing; deploy-drift gates); liveness treated as fund safety (refund-not-strand; skip-not-panic on adversary content).

## 7. Accepted residual risks at GO

If every box is checked, freeze is approved; any KAT/gate failure is a hard blocker.

## 9. Conclusion
Value- and consensus-critical surface exceptionally engineered and free of exploitable defects to the extent a rigorous manual review can establish. Remaining work is confirmation, not investigation. On the Section 8 conditions, approved for freeze and deployment.
