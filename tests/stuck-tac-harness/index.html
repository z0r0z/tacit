AssertionExpectedActual

Gross accounting. y = A − floor(A × 30 / 10000) − 0.10 TAC. Baseline gross = starting gross − Σy + Σclean A. Rebalanced gross = baseline gross + ΣE for completed sales only. Neither is quotable capacity.

Exposure accounting. B = E − floor(E × 30 / 10000) − 0.10 TAC, using the greatest exact E on the fee plateau. Funding selection charges daily cap before sale. Eligible main, reverse stock, pending sales and refunds count; unread residual risk does not.

Stylised fixture, not production state-machine evidence. The production reverse service may combine notes and return change, while this economics fixture uses whole-note sales. Lease admission, maxNote, maxInventory, maxOpen, maxLocked and exact pool note-cut costs are outside this model. Native sats costs and actual quotable capacity are excluded, never zeroed. Buyer demand is never guaranteed.
