\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Secret Sats lets you pay in bitcoin without revealing who paid whom, or how much.
A self-custody lock on Bitcoin, backed by an Ethereum escrow, mints cBTC in Tacit's confidential pool,
and zero-knowledge proofs, not signers, carry it back to Bitcoin as a native Tacit asset.
There, every transfer hides its amount and pays a one-time, silent-payment-style address that only the
recipient can find.
Shielding cBTC into Tacit's Bitcoin pool also hides where a payment came from, and relayers post it so
the sender needs no Bitcoin wallet.
Sats enter and leave by atomic swap, the cBTC pilot is live on mainnet, and it all runs on signet today.
\end{abstract}
