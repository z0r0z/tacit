\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Secret Sats brings private payments to Bitcoin: send bitcoin-backed cBTC with the amount hidden, and
through a shielded pool with sender and receiver unlinked.
cBTC is minted against real BTC locked on Bitcoin, secured by an Ethereum escrow, and comes home to
Bitcoin through an SP1 light-client proof, with no bridge multisig in between.
On Bitcoin, every cBTC transfer hides its amount, and stealth addresses give each payment a one-time key
that only the recipient can find.
Shield it into Tacit's Bitcoin pool and zero-knowledge proofs hide where a payment came from too, while
relayers post spends so senders need no Bitcoin wallet at all.
The cBTC pilot is live on mainnet, the pool's shield, private payment and exit already run end to end on
signet, and sats will move in and out by atomic swap, and by covenant the moment Bitcoin allows one.
\end{abstract}
