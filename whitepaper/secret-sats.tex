Secret Sats makes bitcoin payments private, on Bitcoin itself.
Sats mix in equal-amount joins with no coordinator, then swap for cBTC: bitcoin locked on Bitcoin, minted
in Tacit's immutable pool on Ethereum, and carried back by zero-knowledge proofs anyone can generate, with
no multisig in between.
cBTC then moves through a pool that nobody holds, where each payment carries a proof made on the payer's
own device, so amounts, senders and receivers stay hidden.
Every node derives the pool's state from Bitcoin blocks alone, and relayers post payments, so paying needs
no Bitcoin wallet.
It runs on signet today.
