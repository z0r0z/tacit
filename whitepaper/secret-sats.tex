Secret Sats makes bitcoin payments private on Bitcoin itself.
Sats mix in equal-amount joins with no coordinator, then swap for cBTC: bitcoin locked on Bitcoin, minted
in Tacit's immutable pool on Ethereum, and bridged back by zero-knowledge proofs that anyone can generate,
with no multisig in between.
cBTC enters a pool that nobody holds, where each payment carries a proof made on the payer's own device,
and amounts, senders and receivers stay hidden.
Every node derives the pool from Bitcoin blocks alone, and relayers post payments, so the payer needs no
Bitcoin wallet.
It runs on signet today.
