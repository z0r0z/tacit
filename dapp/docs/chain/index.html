Use it

Get started

Everything happens on tacit.finance/tac. Sign in with your Ethereum wallet: one signature makes your Tacit key, and the same wallet always makes the same key, on any device.

  1. Shield. Choose how much TAC to make private and press Shield. Sign once and it goes in with no ETH, or send one transaction yourself if you hold ETH. It shows as private TAC on the Tacit chain. For now one shield takes up to a launch limit, which the page shows.
  2. Send. Enter a tacit1… address, a name.wei or an 0x… address, and an amount. It says Sent within seconds and settles on Ethereum in about a minute. To a tacit1 address or a name, the payment stays private. To an 0x address it arrives as public TAC, and the sender stays hidden.
  3. Get paid. The Receive tab has your tacit1 address, a pay link such as tacit.finance/to/yourname/25, and a code to scan that turns to Paid when the money arrives.
  4. Withdraw. Take TAC out to your wallet as public TAC, into the DeFi pool to swap, farm or borrow privately, or to Bitcoin.

A tacit1 address can be paid on the chain once its owner has opened their Tacit wallet. Private TAC already in the DeFi pool moves onto the chain from the same page.

Use it

Fees

Every action pays a small fee in TAC, shown before you press. Most of it pays the leader for Ethereum gas, so it moves with gas prices. A fixed part stays in the chain’s pool.

ActionStays in the poolPays for gas
Send0.002 TACThe leader’s fee
ShieldA small margin, now 0.02%The leader’s fee, and the cost of sending it in when you sign once
Withdraw0.1%, at least 0.002 TACThe leader’s fee

Your private balance is a number of shares of that pool. What stays in it raises the worth of every share, so each payment others make adds a little to what yours is worth. A share’s worth in TAC never goes down.

Use it

If something goes wrong

  • The leader stops. After 10 minutes with no block, anyone may land the next one.
  • Your payment is left out. Press “Post it to Ethereum yourself” in the app. Every block must take it within an hour. A posted payment shows on Ethereum which notes it spends and makes; the amounts stay hidden.
  • A shield is never taken in. After 7 days, press Take back in the app. Your TAC returns to your wallet, or as private TAC in the DeFi pool.
  • You lose your device. Sign in with the same wallet anywhere. Your key is the same, and your notes are found again from what is on Ethereum.

How it works

A payment’s path

  1. Your deviceProves the payment. Keys and amounts stay on it.
  2. LeaderChecks the proof, answers in seconds, gathers a block.
  3. ProverAny GPU on the prover board. One proof for the whole block.
  4. EthereumChecks that proof, keeps the block’s data, holds the TAC.

Your device makes a zero-knowledge proof (Halo2, the circuit of the Bitcoin shielded pool) that you own the notes you spend and that the amounts add up. The leader checks it and puts it in the next block. A prover runs the block program in SP1: it checks every proof in the block in one pairing, applies the block, and wraps the result in one Groth16 proof. TacitBlocks.land checks that proof on Ethereum and records the block.

Checking a block’s proof costs Ethereum the same for one payment or thirty, so the payments in a block share it.

How it works

Notes and shares

  • Notes. A balance is a set of notes. Each is a commitment to an amount and an owner’s key, a leaf of the note tree. The tree holds only commitments: no amount or owner is in it.
  • Nullifiers. Spending a note publishes its nullifier, a tag only its owner can compute. It stops a second spend without saying which note was spent.
  • Spends. A spend takes up to two notes and makes up to three: what is paid, your change and the leader’s fee.
  • Memos. Each new note carries a 1,152-byte encrypted memo. Your wallet finds your notes by opening memos with your key. The encryption pairs ML-KEM-768 with secp256k1, so a recorded memo stays private even against a future quantum computer.
  • Shares. Notes count shares of one pool. A share is worth (pool + 1) / (shares + 1) TAC, the 1 being a virtual TAC no note holds. A shield buys shares at that rate, a withdrawal sells them back less its fee, and what fees leave in the pool raises the rate.

How it works

Blocks and leaders

A leader takes payments and lands blocks. Turns come from TacitSeats, which has no owner.

  • Seats. Anyone can take a seat by locking 1,000 TAC. Leaving returns it 7 days later.
  • Turns. Time runs in slots of 50 Ethereum blocks (about 10 minutes), 16 slots to an epoch. Each slot one seat leads, drawn with Ethereum’s beacon randomness. While no seat is held, the bootstrap leader leads.
  • Pay. Each payment carries a fee note to its leader, which covers the gas of landing. Provers are paid in points.
  • No one can stall it. After 10 minutes with no block anyone may land, and whatever is posted to the forced queue must be in a block within an hour.

To lead, run the node and leader from tacit-leader ↗ and call announce(url). To prove blocks on your GPU, see tacit.finance/prove.

The contract has no owner, no upgrade key and no pause. A new design comes as a new chain, and each user moves their own notes to it, with their privacy.

A block’s prover sees which nullifiers and new notes came from the same payment, within that block. It never sees keys, amounts or owners. The proofs use pairings, as most zk-rollups on Ethereum do. The memos are post-quantum already.

Build on it

The leader’s API is public and needs no key.

curl https://tacit-chain-leader.onrender.com/state   # height, roots, pool and shares
curl https://tacit-chain-leader.onrender.com/fee     # what a spend pays now
RouteGives
GET /stateHeight, note root, counts, the forced queue, pool and shares
GET /feeThe fee, the shield’s sweep fee and cap, the leader’s chain address
GET /notes?from=N&limit=MNew notes and their memos, to scan
GET /path/NA note’s Merkle path
GET /spent/0x…Whether a nullifier is spent
POST /spendHands over a proved spend; follow it at /receipt/ID
POST /forcedThe bytes TacitBlocks.force takes

In JavaScript, dapp/tacit-chain-client.js is the app’s whole chain wallet. Amounts are units of 10−8 TAC.

import { makeChainClient } from './dapp/tacit-chain-client.js';
import { tacitChain } from './dapp/tacit-chain-config.js';
import { makeHalo2System } from './dapp/btc-pool-halo2-prover.js';

// rpcCall(method, params): an Ethereum JSON-RPC call. priv: the Tacit key
const system = makeHalo2System({ wasm, params, vk, pinnedVkHash });
const c = makeChainClient({ cfg: tacitChain(), rpcCall, priv, system: () => system });
await c.ready();
await c.scan();                     // find your notes
await c.worth();                    // your balance, in units
await c.pay(chainAddress, units);   // proved here, handed to the leader
await c.withdraw('0x…', units);
