    How it works

    The pool holds Tacit tokens: test cBTC, a bitcoin-backed token, on signet; TAC and cBTC on mainnet. Sats can mix first, with no coordinator, then swap into cBTC. cBTC is shielded into the pool, paid privately inside it, and withdrawn the same way.

    Questions

    What is this?

    A working demo of private payments on Bitcoin. Send sats, pay a silent address, mix sats with other wallets into equal coins with no history, or move cBTC through a shielded pool that hides amounts and owners.

    Is it a sidechain?

    No. Every step is an ordinary Bitcoin transaction. Indexers replay Bitcoin and check each proof; there is no other chain, federation or operator.

    Where are my sats? What is cBTC?

    Sats and silent payments stay sats in your wallet. On signet, the pool holds cBTC, a Tacit token backed by locked BTC and a slashable escrow; sats turn into cBTC and back to sats by atomic swap, no custodian either way. On mainnet the pool carries TAC and cBTC.

    Who can see what?

    Anyone sees that a pool transaction happened. Only payer and payee know the amount and who paid whom. Silent and shielded addresses give each payment a fresh output only the recipient can find.

    Do I need to trust anyone?

    Bitcoin, the proof system, and the indexer you use — run your own and it is just Bitcoin and the proofs. No one holds the pool: a note moves only with its owner’s proof.

    Does proving cost anything?

    No. Your device proves each shield, payment and withdrawal in seconds, after a one-time download of the prover. You pay the Bitcoin fee, or, on mainnet, the relay’s fee when it posts a payment for you.

    Mainnet or signet?

    Mainnet; signet testing is switched off. Sends, silent payments and the shielded pool all run on mainnet, where the pool carries TAC and cBTC. A relay posts private payments for a fee in the token paid, so your Bitcoin address does not show as the sender; shielding and withdrawing are posted from your own Bitcoin address and pay its Bitcoin fee. The signet demo, with a test cBTC from a faucet, has been switched off.
