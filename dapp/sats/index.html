    How it works

    The pool holds cBTC, a bitcoin-backed token. Sats swap into cBTC, cBTC is shielded into the pool, paid privately inside it, and leaves the same way.

    • Amounts inside the pool

    • Which note was spent

    • What entered and what exited

    Questions

    What is this?

    A working demo of private payments on Bitcoin: send sats, pay a silent address, or move test cBTC through a shielded pool that hides amounts and owners.

    Is it a sidechain?

    No. Every step is an ordinary Bitcoin transaction. Indexers replay Bitcoin and check each proof; there is no other chain, federation or operator.

    Where are my sats? What is cBTC?

    Sats and silent payments stay sats in your wallet. The pool holds cBTC, a Tacit token backed by locked BTC and a slashable escrow; sats become cBTC and back by atomic swap. On signet it is a test token.

    Who can see what?

    Anyone sees that a pool transaction happened. Only payer and payee know the amount and who paid whom. Silent and shielded addresses give each payment a fresh output only the recipient can find.

    Do I need to trust anyone?

    Bitcoin, the proof system and the indexer you use; run your own and it is Bitcoin and the proofs. No one holds the pool: a note moves only with its owner’s proof.

    Does proving cost anything?

    No. Your device proves each shield, payment and exit in seconds, after a one-time download of the prover. You pay only the Bitcoin fee.

    Mainnet or signet?

    Sends and silent payments run on mainnet today. The pool runs on signet with a development setup; mainnet follows a public multi-party setup ceremony.
