    How it works

    Two chains, one state

    Each chain keeps a proven copy of the other’s Tacit state. We call it reflection.

    • No federation
    • No multisig
    • No soft fork
    • Live on mainnet
    • Anyone can run it

    Reflection

    1. Bitcoin, inEvery block, once 24 deep.
    2. Ethereum, inIts finalized state, checked by a light client.
    3. One proof, outVerified by the pool. Anyone can make it.
    The bridge

    Burn, prove, mint

    1. 1 · BurnThe note is burned on Bitcoin. Amount hidden.The note is burned in the pool. Amount hidden.
    2. 2 · ProveOnce 24 blocks deep, the burn joins Bitcoin’s proof.Once Ethereum finalizes, the cross-out joins Bitcoin’s proof.
    3. 3 · MintThe pool mints a fresh note of the same value, once.A Bitcoin transaction mints a fresh note of the same value, once.
    The note

    One format, two chains

    A Tacit note is a Pedersen commitment on secp256k1, the curve both chains use.

    C = v·H + r·G · amount v hidden, blinding r known only to its owner.

    Step by step

    1. Headers, by workA relay on Ethereum checks every Bitcoin header’s proof of work.
    2. Every envelope foldedA program folds every Tacit envelope in each block.
    3. One proofSP1 proves the fold, 24 or more blocks behind the relay’s tip.
    4. Checked by the poolThe pool verifies it against a pinned key and mints each burn once.

    3. Inside Bitcoin’s proofBitcoin’s program verifies the Ethereum proof recursively.
    4. Folded on BitcoinA cross-out mints on Bitcoin once, only if proven a member.

    Trust

    Who checks what

    No multisig, federation or attestor set signs a bridge message. You rely on Bitcoin’s proof of work, Ethereum’s validators, and SP1 proofs with verifying keys anyone can rebuild.

    Proof of work

    A relay on Ethereum checks every header’s work. A reorg deeper than 24 blocks halts reflection.

    The sync committee’s signatures

    Checked inside the proof by an sp1-helios light client, from a pinned anchor, bound to Ethereum’s genesis.

    An immutable contract

    Every proof is verified against pinned verifying keys that nobody can change.

    Bitcoin reflection 0x00bb158b…074bc5
    Ethereum reflection 0x00ca8171…6aea72
    Bitcoin orders, the rules decide

    Ops ride in Taproot witnesses. The same rules over the same chain give every indexer the same state, which reflection proves.

    Reproducible programs

    Each program rebuilds byte for byte from source, pinned by SHA-256 to its verifying key.

    Good to know

    Before you cross

    Can anyone see how much I bridge?

    The burn and the mint are public, but neither chain publishes the amount. Only whoever proves the mint sees it, which today is Tacit’s prover. A cBTC lock’s size is visible.

    Is there a limit?

    While the bridge is new, per bridge: TAC 20 to 1,000 to Bitcoin and up to 1,000 back; tETH 0.001 to 0.5 to Bitcoin and up to 0.5 back; cBTC 0.00001 to 0.5 to Bitcoin and up to 0.5 back; cUSD 10 to 1,000 to Bitcoin and up to 1,000 back; cUSDT 2 to 100 to Bitcoin and up to 100 back. A note goes back whole, so split a larger one first. TAC that Bitcoin’s proof has not reached moves in two steps, a move and then a burn, each waiting for a proof, usually within a day. Up to 255 transfers of a note’s history are proven.

    What does it cost?

    From Bitcoin you pay the Bitcoin network fee, and Tacit covers the Ethereum mint for now. To Bitcoin, a small relay fee comes out of the note and the Bitcoin fee, in sats, is paid from your Bitcoin address. Each cost shows before you sign.

    Is there a faster way?

    For TAC from Bitcoin, yes: the fast bridge pays you private TAC from a provider’s own in about 15 minutes, for the provider’s fee. Your TAC is locked on Bitcoin until the provider pays, and comes back to you after about a day if it does not. Anyone with private TAC can be a provider there.

    Can I close the page?

    Yes, nothing is lost. A step that needs your key, such as the burn of a note that was moved to its burn address, waits until you open the page with your wallet. A note that was moved but not burned can also be moved back.

    I cleared my browser. Is my bridge lost?

    No. A bridge is rebuilt from your key. Open tacit.finance with your key and it is found again from your Bitcoin address shortly after the page opens, whether it was moved or burned, and TAC that has arrived is in your private balance. TAC sent privately to your tacit1 address and TAC you bridged to Bitcoin are looked for once more within a minute. If one is still not listed, add it with its burn or move transaction id under “A bridge you started is not listed” on the Bridge tab.
