How each one stays private

BTC

Sent as a silent payment (BIP‑352): each payment lands at a fresh address only the recipient’s key can find, and nothing on chain ties it to their published address. The amount and your own Bitcoin address show. Spending it from here combines it with your Bitcoin address’s coins.

TAC

Paid inside the shielded pool on Bitcoin, with a zero-knowledge proof made on this device. Posted by the relay for a fee in TAC, it hides who paid, who was paid and how much; posted from your own Bitcoin address, that address shows as the sender.

ETH

Paid inside the private pool on Ethereum, Base or Robinhood Chain, proved on this device and sent by a relay: no gas, and nothing on chain links payer and payee. Deposits and withdrawals show.
