How each one stays private

BTC

Sent as a silent payment (BIP‑352): each one lands at a fresh address only the recipient’s key can find. The amount and your Bitcoin address show.

TAC

Paid inside the shielded pool, proved on this device. Who paid, who was paid and how much stay hidden; posted from your own Bitcoin address instead, it shows as the sender.

ETH

Paid inside the private pool on four chains, proved on this device, with no network fee. Nothing on chain links payer and payee; deposits and withdrawals show.
