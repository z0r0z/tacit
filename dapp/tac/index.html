Your Tacit address

The one to share. Tacit apps pay it inside the shielded pool, and the same address takes BTC and ETH. It says nothing about what you hold, and each payment to it arrives at a different one-time key that only your wallet can find.

Your pool address

The pool part alone, for an app that asks for a bp1… address. What it receives lands in the same shielded balance.

What is and isn't public

  • PublicThat something happened, and in which Bitcoin block. How many notes the pool holds.
  • HiddenEvery amount, inside the pool and at both edges. Which note was spent, and who was paid. What you hold.
  • Not even usNobody publishes the pool's total, because no amount in it is ever published. Only you can add up your own notes.
  • TrustNo custodian, federation or multisig. Proofs check against a key pinned in this page, and the indexer replays Bitcoin itself rather than trusting a feed.
