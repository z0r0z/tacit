What is and is not public

  • PublicThat something happened, in which Bitcoin block, and how many notes the pool holds.
  • HiddenEvery amount, in the pool and at both edges. Which note was spent, who was paid, what you hold.
  • TrustNo custodian, federation or multisig. Proofs check against a key pinned in this page, and the indexer replays Bitcoin.
