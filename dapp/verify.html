    TAC on Ethereum

    TAC’s public ERC-20 is minted only by the Tacit pool, from TAC proven to have left Bitcoin, and burned when it is wrapped back in. These checks read Ethereum directly: every value must come back the same from two public nodes.

    With a light client or a full node running on this computer, tick this and the page asks only that node, once for each value. The public nodes are not asked. The node has to allow this page’s origin (CORS), and your browser may ask before letting a page reach a service on this computer.

      Beyond supply: conservation in circulation

      A fixed supply at genesis is one property. Conservation since then is another: a spend cannot create TAC. Tacit’s indexer checks it by walking a Bitcoin output’s ancestry backward, hop by hop, through the protocol’s transfer opcodes, re-verifying the Pedersen-commitment conservation and the kernel signature at each hop until it reaches this same etch.

      That code is worker/src/tac-ancestry.js. It is open source, runs against the same public Bitcoin data this page uses and takes no shortcut through an index. Point it at any TAC-carrying outpoint: it needs only a txid and a vout.

      Emissions

      The supply is fixed at the etch; these programs only move existing TAC to people.

      First airdrop (Bitcoin). A snapshot of holders of two Ethereum tokens, paid out on Bitcoin. Holder lists: airdrop/.

      Second airdrop (Ethereum). 999,999 TAC to holders of seven tokens, claimable until 2026-12-20 through TacAirdrop. Snapshot and method: airdrop/v1/.

      Points. 100,000 TAC over 90 days to 2026-12-21, split daily by protocol use, claimed through PointsDistributor. Rules: README.

      Farms. 99,700 TAC over 90 days to 2026-12-19 for liquidity providers, through FarmManager and PrecisionFarm. Details: FARMS.md.
