How the figures are counted
Transacted
ETH deposited into the Tacit pool (its Wrap events) and into the device pools on (their Transact events). Every deposit counts, so ETH that goes in and comes out again is counted each time it goes in; Private now is what the pools hold.
Private now
The ETH balance of the Tacit pool and of each device pool.
Withdrawn
What those pools paid out: withdrawals and relay fees.
Wallets
Distinct senders of the Tacit pool’s wraps. Device pools also take deposits through routers and keepers, so their senders are not counted.
By pool
Each pool’s own deposits, payments out and balance. The totals are the figures above. Withdrawn includes relay fees.
ETH over time
Private now: each pool’s deposits and payments out, added up as they happened, ending on its balance. Beneath it, the ETH transacted on each UTC day, to its own scale.
BTC locked
Proven locks not yet spent or redeemed, each found by its bond (EscrowPosted).
cBTC minted
Every mint on those locks, including locks since closed.
Bonds
wstETH in escrow now.
cUSD borrowed
Totals from the CdpMinted, CdpClosed and CdpLiquidated events. Open is the engine’s outstanding debt.
Borrowed against TAC
Each vault’s total debt: TACD from the dollar vault, TACE from the ether vault. TAC deposited is the TAC the two vaults hold. Read from the vaults on each visit.
TAC ERC-20
Its supply. The pool mints it when TAC is made public and burns it when TAC is made private, so private TAC is not in it.
TAC made public
TAC the pool has minted to an account: paid out of a private note by a withdrawal, or from its own reserve when someone buys TAC on its public market. Left out: relay fee mints, and the treasury the protocol bridged at the start (to the ops multisig and the airdrop contract).
Between the chains
What passed between Bitcoin and Ethereum, drawn when it happened: proofs of Bitcoin landing on Ethereum, bridge burns recorded and minted, cross-outs to Bitcoin, TAC made public and private, ETH deposits and settles. A counter with no time of its own is drawn when it moved, to the nearest reading. Counts and times only.
Airdrop claimed
The airdrop’s Claimed events.
Points
Every account on the points leaderboard. TAC allocated and claimed are read from the points contract.
Bitcoin headers
The Bitcoin light relay’s tip, and the blocks relayed since its anchor.
Proven to
The Bitcoin block the pool last attested to.
Settles
Transactions that made or spent notes.
Cross-chain
Moves out to Bitcoin (and how many are folded into its state), Bitcoin notes spent on Ethereum, and bridges in: Bitcoin TAC burned and recorded by the reflection, then minted on Ethereum by its holder. Bridges in counts the ones minted; burns recorded and not yet minted are named beside it.
Sources
Each chain’s own nodes, with transaction lists from Blockscout and Routescan. Refreshed every 15 minutes.
