How it's counted
Made private
ETH deposited into the Tacit pool (its Wrap events) and into the device pools on Ethereum, Base and Robinhood (their Transact events).
Private now
The ETH balance of the Tacit pool and of each device pool.
Withdrawn
All those pools have paid out: withdrawals and relay fees.
Wallets
Distinct senders of the Tacit pool's wraps. The device pools take deposits through routers and keepers as well as wallets, so their senders aren't counted.
By pool
Each pool's own balance, deposits and payments out; the totals are the figures above. Out includes relay fees.
BTC locked
Proven locks not yet spent or redeemed, each found by its bond (EscrowPosted).
cBTC minted
Every mint on those locks, including on locks since closed.
Bonds
wstETH in escrow now.
cUSD
CdpMinted, CdpClosed and CdpLiquidated events; open is the engine's outstanding debt.
TAC ERC-20
Its supply. The pool mints it as TAC is withdrawn and burns it as TAC is made private, so private TAC isn't in it.
Airdrop claimed
The airdrop's Claimed events.
Points
Every account on the points leaderboard; TAC allocated and claimed from the points distributor.
Bitcoin headers
The light relay's tip, and blocks relayed since its anchor.
Proven to
The Bitcoin block the pool last attested to.
Settles
Transactions that made or spent notes.
Cross-chain
Moves out to Bitcoin (and how many are folded into its state), Bitcoin notes spent on Ethereum, and bridges in: Bitcoin TAC burned and recorded by the reflection, each minted on Ethereum by its holder.
Sources
Each chain's own nodes, with transaction lists from Blockscout and Routescan. Refreshed every quarter hour.
